LUKA'S LETTER TO SANTA - PROJECT DESCRIPTION

Luka is 18 months old. This is her Christmas list, and she cannot write it
herself, so we are writing it for her.

We are building a small, interactive web app for our family (grandparents,
aunts, uncles, godparents). It does two things.

1. A letter from Luka to Santa
   A mock-up letter written in Luka's voice, in Lithuanian. It is funny and
   warm, and it tells Santa that she was a very good girl this year and only
   sometimes naughty (with a few honest examples). It looks handwritten by
   Luka, on cream paper with a red and green candy-stripe border, with small
   Santa and nutcracker drawings.

2. A shared present list
   A list of presents Luka would really like, each with a picture, a short
   note, and a link to an example in a shop. Family members pick their name
   from a list and tick a box to reserve a gift. A reservation is saved and
   shown as "reserved" to everyone who opens the app, so the same gift is never
   bought twice (no ten Lego sets for Luka). Only the person who reserved a
   gift can cancel it. Parents do not see who reserved what in the final app;
   a test mode shows it before launch so we can check that it works.

Who it is for: close family, mostly on phones. Many are not very technical, so
it must be simple: open the link, read the letter, pick a gift.

What success looks like:
- Family members can open the app from a single link, with no account needed.
- Everyone sees the same, up-to-date list of reserved and free gifts.
- Two people cannot reserve the same gift at the same time.
- Nobody ends up buying a duplicate gift.

Built by Milda and Justas as the Xmas project in the Turing College
"Building with AI" course, using Claude Code.
